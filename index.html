About

I started drawing around the same time anyone else does, scribbling with crayons and delighting in paint. My focus on people became exciting when I was 11, and drew a portrait of my classmate from the side, catching something about the way it felt to see my friend. This continued to be a fun way to cope with the intensity of rubrics, hours, expectations, and sitting quietly throughout public school. I'd draw my teachers as they waved their arms around, and study the way light reflected off the hair of the student sitting in front of me.
Portraiture is a curious way to satisfy my interest in the worlds other people live in, perspectives taken for granted that I could hardly conceptulize. Drawing people live is the ultimate way to do this -- as we sit together, something falls away, like a meditation, and we talk about whatever fills the air. Capturing what makes a person feel like a person on the page is a delight and a priviledge to attempt. Thank you to every one of the many wandering souls who have had to courage to sit and be seen.
